## Featured Project

### [Payments Incident Response Agent](https://github.com/anibal-tech/payments-incident-response-agent)

AI-assisted workflow for payment incident analysis and executive incident reporting, orchestrated with n8n.

The project explores how Generative AI can support payment incident response through impact classification, contextual analysis, RCA hypothesis generation, ownership mapping, action planning, and executive reporting while preserving explicit human authority over consequential operational decisions.

The architecture separates three responsibilities:

- **AI reasoning** for analysis, hypotheses, recommendations, and summarization.
- **Deterministic logic** for normalization, context assembly, ownership mapping, approval evaluation, and workflow-state routing.
- **Human authority** for severity validation, RCA confirmation, production actions, external communications, and incident decisions.

The project also includes explicit Responsible AI controls for uncertainty preservation, causal overreach, sensitive payment data, invented operational parameters, and autonomous execution.

**Key areas:** Payments · Incident Management · Generative AI · n8n · Human-in-the-Loop · Responsible AI · Workflow Automation

[View repository](https://github.com/anibal-tech/payments-incident-response-agent)

## Applied AI Approach

Generative AI is an increasingly important part of this portfolio, but the objective is not to apply AI to every problem.

The projects explore where AI can provide practical value in areas such as:

- information classification and summarization,
- retrieval and contextual reasoning,
- hypothesis generation,
- management and operational reporting,
- decision support,
- workflow augmentation.

For business-critical scenarios, I am particularly interested in architectures that combine:

```text
AI Reasoning
+
Deterministic Controls
+
Human Decision Authority
```

This approach allows AI to support analysis and execution without assuming authority over decisions that require operational accountability.
